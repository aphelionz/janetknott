The Great Elephant Migration on Commonwealth Avenue

August 15, 2026

by Janet Knott

It has been said that elephants and people have some things in common. They make lifelong friendships, have good memories, appreciate communal decision making, and remember landscapes. Currently a herd of them has made its way to the Commonwealth Ave. Mall, between Fairfield and Dartmouth Streets. Become part of the herd and check them out, until September 13th.
