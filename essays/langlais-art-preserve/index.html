The Langlais Art Preserve in Cushing

August 26, 2026

by Janet Knott

Driving through Cushing, Maine, I stumbled upon the Langlais Art Preserve, protected by the Georges River Land Trust. Bernard Langlais (1921-1977) had a deep appreciation for the New England seasons, Cushing, the climate, and his art. Here you will find his incredible work, carved out of wood and painted in rich detail. Meander along the paths, and be transported to an otherworldly place.
