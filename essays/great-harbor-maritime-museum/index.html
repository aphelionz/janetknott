The Great Harbor Maritime Museum in Northeast Harbor

August 21, 2026

by Janet Knott

For tourists and locals alike, the Great Harbor Maritime Museum is a must see! A former firehouse, the building has been renovated to offer exhibits featuring the history of the surrounding bays, coves, and harbors. “Chilly Chronicles” documents the history of the ice industry, and out front, the mussel man has a lot of fans.
